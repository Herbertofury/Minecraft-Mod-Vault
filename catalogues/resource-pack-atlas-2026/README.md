## What changed

The first edition was intentionally centered on a Forge 1.20.1 pack. This edition removes that hard gate. A beautiful or uniquely useful pack is now retained and classified by **conversion / port lane** instead:

- Native / drop-in
- Simple pack-format conversion
- Texture-path remap
- Model / EMF / ETF / CIT conversion
- PBR/material conversion
- Mod-version asset port
- Custom-pack-required gap

This keeps old giants such as Faithful 32x Modded and Soartex Fanver Modded as searchable asset mines while also surfacing tiny current projects that would otherwise be missed.

## Massive-scour standouts

- **Bare Bones x Mods** — 60+ mod visual compatibility in one actively maintained pack.
- **Farmer GUI** — 190+ supported mod interfaces; massive visual-cohesion layer for huge modpacks.
- **Bibliophilia** — huge modded enchanted-book compatibility library covering Ars, Apotheosis, Alex’s mods, BetterEnd/Nether, Bewitchment, Malum and much more.
- **Motschen’s Better Leaves** — broad foliage compatibility across Ars, BOP, Regions Unexplored, BetterEnd, Quark, Aether, Fruits Delight, Forbidden & Arcanus, Chipped, Eternal Starlight and more.
- **F.M.R.P** — carries Fresh Animations-style behavior into modded entities through EMF/ETF.
- **Ars Loafers / Ars Naturae** — two very different but excellent Ars Nouveau ecosystem treatments.
- **Mystical 3DRetextured+**, **Croptopia Blocky Crops**, **Crops 3D Reploughed**, and fruit-tree leaf packs — high-value farming/flora fixes.
- **Pinnacle** and **Chaotix Apotheotic Tweaks** — strong full Apotheosis alternatives beyond Excalibur.
