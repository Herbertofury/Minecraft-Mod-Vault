Security and truthfulness boundaries
------------------------------------
- Originals are preserved and mutations occur in disposable working copies or recoverable quarantine transactions.
- Source ZIP traversal, symbolic links, duplicate paths, entry-count/expanded-size abuse, suspicious compression, and extraction escapes are blocked.
- Automatic source edits are limited to recognized version, loader, mapping, Java, metadata, and pack-format fields.
- Build/test/clean execution is restricted to a detected project wrapper and fixed action set, with explicit acknowledgement, dedicated caches, sanitized environment, timeout/cancel, logs, and post-run immutable-source verification.
- The release does not claim operating-system or virtual-machine isolation for arbitrary build scripts.
- A metadata edit, remap, decompile, successful build, or startup is not described as a completed semantic port without target-runtime evidence.
