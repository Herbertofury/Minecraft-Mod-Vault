## 🔄 Important 2026 lineage changes

### Feather → Dawn

**[Feather Client](https://feathermc.com/)** now states that Feather has been acquired by InPvP and users are being moved to **[Dawn](https://dawn.gg/)**. Keep Feather as historical identity/provenance, but use Dawn for the current recommendation and download path.

### PojavLauncher lineage → AngelAuraMC / Amethyst

The former PojavLauncher team site now redirects into **[AngelAuraMC](https://angelauramc.dev/)**. The project describes **Amethyst** as its current prime Minecraft Java launcher for Android and iOS, built from the team's PojavLauncher experience.

### Badlion + Lunar relationship

Badlion remains a distinct client and mod stack, but its 2026 site states that Badlion is also launchable through **[Lunar Client](https://www.lunarclient.com/)**. The Vault therefore tracks both products while avoiding the false impression that their current launcher distribution is completely independent.

## 🔐 Safety notes

- **[SKlauncher](https://skmedix.pl/)** documentation explicitly warns about fake download sites. Use only `skmedix.pl` and its documented beta domain.
- Prefer official project domains or canonical GitHub release pages for every launcher.
- Keep Microsoft/Minecraft authentication inside the launcher's documented OAuth/sign-in flow; never enter credentials into third-party mirror pages.
- For valuable modpacks, use per-instance backups/export before changing launchers so configs, saves, screenshots, options, shaderpacks and resource packs are preserved.
