# 🧠 Compatibility Brain

> **Status:** ✅ Verified in 0.9.0 release evidence.

Compatibility Brain is a durable searchable evidence store for Minecraft versions, loaders, toolchains, porting research and proven repair history.

## Why it matters

A repair that worked once should become **evidence**, not folklore—but it also must not poison unrelated projects. Every reusable rule should stay attached to exact versions, loaders, applicability and proof.

## 0.9.0 evidence snapshot

The release documentation records:

- SQLite in WAL mode;
- FTS5 search;
- hundreds of Minecraft/version/toolchain knowledge records;
- durable Repair Brain history;
- restart persistence.

## Roadmap

OmniBridge evolves this into a machine-readable **Migration Knowledge Base** that tracks renamed/removed APIs, registry changes, rendering/networking changes, Java/runtime shifts, mappings transitions and verified replacements.
