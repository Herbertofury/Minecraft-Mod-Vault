# 🐉 Entities, Behavior & Animation

> **Status:** 📋 OmniBridge roadmap.

## Standalone entity generation

Potential sources include resource-pack replacements, Bedrock entities, Blockbench projects, Java models and other permitted assets.

### Semantic behavior DNA

Instead of copying a donor class forever, OmniBridge can compose behaviors such as:

- grazer
- flee
- pack hunter
- tameable
- ranged attacker
- flying/aquatic navigation
- merchant
- territorial
- neutral-until-provoked

## Rig intelligence

Bone mapping should consider hierarchy, position, orientation, mesh influence, motion and aliases—not names alone. Mappings receive confidence scores and remain editable.

## Animation preservation

Target properties include:

- transforms and pivots;
- keyframes/interpolation;
- timing and looping;
- state/controller conditions;
- events, sounds and particles;
- blending and retargeting.

Low-confidence retargets should open directly in the appropriate review/editor view.
