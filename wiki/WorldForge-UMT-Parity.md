# 🧰 WorldForge vs Universal Minecraft Tool

> **Status:** 📋 Parity/surpass contract. UMT is a benchmark, not a dependency claim.

WorldForge must maintain a living capability ledger against current Universal Minecraft Tool documentation and prove parity/superiority with legal fixture worlds.

## Parity targets

### NBT / saved-data editing

- visual chunk locator;
- biome tinting and block-state rendering;
- search;
- copy/paste;
- undo/redo;
- hex/raw data;
- player resolution;
- multi-world editing;
- preservation of unknown data;
- extension/schema assistance.

### Converter

- broad Java/Bedrock/legacy-version coverage where verified;
- no artificial world-size limit;
- terrain, biomes, items, entities and block entities;
- partially generated chunks;
- older-chunk migration;
- depth/height/world-center transforms;
- block-property recalculation;
- lighting and heightmaps;
- multithreaded conversion.

### Pruner

- Box / Circle / Polygon / Split selections;
- inhabited-time filters;
- huge low-memory deletion;
- multi-layer selections;
- Bedrock LevelDB optimization;
- exact preview and safety copy.

- structure metadata and invisible gameplay systems;
- modded world + required-content migration;

## Rule

WorldForge cannot claim “better than UMT” globally until representative fixtures prove it.
