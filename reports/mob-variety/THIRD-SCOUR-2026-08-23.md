# Minecraft Mob Variety - Third Delta Scour

**Research date:** 2026-08-23  
**Baseline:** 171 verified projects  
**Qualified additions:** 82  
**New master total:** **253 unique projects**

This pass was deliberately **delta-only** against the verified 171-project Drive tracker. Existing entries were not re-added under alternate spellings or mirrors. Tiny behavior-only tweaks, compatibility patches, obvious near-duplicates, and projects with too little public evidence were scanned but excluded from the count.
