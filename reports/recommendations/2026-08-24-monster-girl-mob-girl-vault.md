## Safety split

Gameplay mods such as Monster Girl, HMaG, NFF: Girls and Grimoire of Gaia add persistent content or relationship/gameplay state and should be version-tested with backups. CEM/resource-pack projects such as PitonixRex's conversions and Cute Mob Models are presentation layers and are normally high-confidence removable/world-safe, although exact renderer and version requirements still need to match the client.
